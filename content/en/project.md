## ⚽ RoboCup Junior Soccer
---
- In RoboCup Junior Soccer, a competition where two fully autonomous robots play soccer against each other, I was mainly involved in the design and development of the robot's hardware. Over approximately three years of design and development, our team achieved **5th place nationwide** at the Japan national tournament.
- [[Match Video](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLkEBRGnKNUILFJv4zKvQkQi69NoT-_FYg)], [[Robot Details](https://note.com/spinach_egg/n/n5938fe6f424b)], [[Board Data](https://github.com/takeyamayuki/RCJ_Japan_Soccer2017_Board)], [[RoboCupJuniorJapan HP](https://www.robocupjunior.jp/index.html)], [[RoboCupJuniorJapan Competition Results](https://www.robocupjunior.jp/docs/RCJJ2017Results-Soccer_LightWeight(WSL).pdf)], [[High School Science Research Presentation Winners List](https://www.cfs.chiba-u.jp/koudai-renkei/event/history/2016/10jusyou.pdf)]
- **Technical Skills**: Arduino, C++, 3DCAD, KiCad, Sensors (IMU, Ultrasonic sensor, Infrared sensor).
- **Soft Skills**: Teamwork, Time Management, Communication, Presentation skills.

## 👆 NonMouse
---
- I developed a software called "NonMouse." NonMouse allows users to operate a computer without a physical mouse by recognizing hand movements from a webcam. I received the **Excellent Award** in the GEEKTEN Development Skill Support category. It was also selected as **Zenn Weekly Top** and was **nominated for the INNO-vation Program 2023 "Generation Award."**
- [[GitHub](https://github.com/takeyamayuki/NonMouse2)], [[Zenn](https://zenn.dev/ninzin/articles/94b05fdb9edf53)], [[GEEKTEN 2021 HP](https://talent.supporterz.jp/geekten/2021/)], [[GEEKTEN 2021 Certificate of Commendation](https://drive.google.com/file/d/13QC0B-ERtwVyCp4O0RPveOUX1KNsMWpP/view?usp=sharing)], [[INNO-vation Program 2023 Nominees List](https://www.inno.go.jp/result/2023/generation/nominate/)], [[INNO-vation Program 2023 Certificate of Nomination](https://drive.google.com/file/d/12GZg1Wx3ZPSXtmn1RosNp5PXvozhiQMS/view?usp=sharing)]
- **Technical Skills**: Python (with OpenCV, MediaPipe, Numpy, Tkinter), Ubuntu, Git/GitHub.
- **Soft Skills**: Time Management, Presentation skills, Communication.

## 👣 ASHIMO
---
- In the Embedded Development Programming course at the University of Tsukuba Graduate School, I developed a system that allows users to pass through ticket gates hands-free by authenticating with RFID tags attached to their feet. The project won **1st place** at the final presentation of the course.
- [[GitHub](https://github.com/takeyamayuki/ASHIMO)], [[Video](https://drive.google.com/file/d/125ItnGQid4D2cwPhSFbauSmri3XzdNFZ/view?usp=sharing)]
- **Technical Skills**: Android Studio, Java, Firebase, Git/GitHub.
- **Soft Skills**: Teamwork, Time Management, Communication, Presentation skills.
